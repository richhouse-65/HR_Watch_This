## Background

Self-taught, focused entirely on networking. I work on Kali Linux and spend most of my time understanding how traffic moves, how devices communicate, and how to diagnose when something breaks. I came from a brief period in cybersecurity but shifted to networking because it is what actually interests me — the architecture, the protocols, the way machines find each other across a network the same way people communicate across languages.

Currently building toward a role as a NOC technician. Everything in this portfolio reflects tools I use regularly and understand at a practical level.
